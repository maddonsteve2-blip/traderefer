## Distilled Notes

This video presents a full local SEO workflow: entity research, Google Business Profile category/service audit, gap analysis, topical relevance planning, geographic relevance planning, multi-pass content generation, schema, images, video, internal/external links, and publishing.

Important takeaways:

- Start with entity research: primary category, secondary categories, services, competitors, and missing coverage.
- Build the "Core 30" around real categories and services. The site should mirror the Google Business Profile hierarchy.
- Topical relevance proves the business does the service. Geographic relevance proves the business serves the place.
- Supporting content should come from real search and buyer questions, competitor headings, forums, PAA results, and local context.
- Local content needs specifics from the area, not generic service copy.
- The writing workflow should be multi-pass: research synthesis, outline, section drafts, rhythm cleanup, anti-generic wording, human open/close, conversion pass, QA.
- Schema, metadata, FAQs, internal links, authority links, and media should be produced alongside the content, not bolted on later.

## TradeRefer Translation

TradeRefer should adapt this as a governance workflow, not as mass autopublishing:

1. Pull candidate pages from GSC and sitemap data.
2. Check if each page has enough real business/profile data to deserve indexing.
3. Build or improve only pages that pass the data gate.
4. Add direct answer blocks, profile evidence, schema, FAQs, and canonical links.
5. Noindex or omit thin pages until they have enough support.

## Actions For TradeRefer

- Build a page eligibility classifier for `/b/`, `/local/`, `/trades/`, and `/top/`.
- Generate local page briefs only after confirming there are real matching businesses.
- Keep generated drafts behind a QA step; never publish AI-generated local pages blindly.
- Use metadata/schema/content generation as a bundled page update, not separate disconnected tasks.
- Focus the first batch on GSC pages already ranking but not clicking.

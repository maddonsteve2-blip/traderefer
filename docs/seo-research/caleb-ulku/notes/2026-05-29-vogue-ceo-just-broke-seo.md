## Distilled Notes

Caleb uses the Conde Nast discussion about budgeting for zero Google traffic to explain the shift from publisher-style search traffic to answer-first search. He distinguishes this from local transactional search, where map packs and direct calls still matter, but argues that local will face a delayed version through AI Overviews and Ask Maps.

Important takeaways:

- Informational/publisher traffic is already under pressure from AI answers and search result layouts.
- Local transactional queries still produce map-pack actions, but AI recommendation layers are becoming more important.
- Google is not simply anti-AI content. The problem is fake, generic, unsupported content that does not reflect a real operator, service, or geography.
- Real local expertise beats mass-generated city pages.
- Good local content should reflect real services, real scenarios, real photos, real reviews, real citations, and actual service areas.
- AI should execute a human strategy, not replace it.

## TradeRefer Translation

TradeRefer is exposed because it has a large programmatic directory footprint. The win is not to hide from AI or stop SEO. The win is to make the indexable footprint look like a real marketplace with useful local evidence, not a pile of variable-swapped pages.

Indexable local pages should show:

- actual matching businesses,
- real service and location coverage,
- page-specific answer blocks,
- clear next action for the user,
- canonical internal links,
- schema that matches visible content.

Pages that cannot show that should be noindexed or omitted from sitemaps until they can.

## Actions For TradeRefer

- Stop expanding local/service combinations until eligibility rules are enforced.
- Use AI to draft briefs and variants, but publish only after data and QA gates pass.
- Add firsthand-style marketplace data where possible: profile count, matching service categories, listed suburbs, referral/click signals, claimed profiles.
- Build "AI answer blocks" at the top of priority local pages: what the page is, who it lists, how to compare, and what to do next.
- Treat Ask Maps/AI assistants as future buyers: they need structured, attribute-rich evidence to recommend TradeRefer pages.

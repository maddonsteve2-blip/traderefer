# Change This To Rank FAST!

- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=myhshBjsi2A
- Published: 2026-05-22
- Transcript pulled: yes, English
- Relevance: local SEO audit, Core 30, schema, conversion layout, reviews, and why generic blogs do not rescue local rankings.

## Distilled Notes

Caleb audits real local service websites and compares strong ranking pages against weaker pages with more reviews or larger footprints. His main point is that ranking and conversion are not solved by page count alone. The winning pages make the business entity, service entity, location entity, and trust signals obvious.

Important takeaways:

- Title tags and H1s must state the real service and location, not vague slogans.
- The first screen should quickly answer why this business should be called, not repeat generic history or "trusted professionals" copy.
- Social proof belongs near the top because it affects call intent, even if it is not the direct ranking factor.
- LocalBusiness and Organization schema should match visible content, including social profiles via `sameAs` where possible.
- Review content matters more when it describes real scenarios, services, and outcomes. Generic "great service" reviews are weak for AI systems.
- The "Core 30" idea is a local site structure that mirrors the Google Business Profile: homepage for the primary category and city, then category/service pages under the same hierarchy.
- Generic blog posts are usually a poor substitute for proper service, category, and location architecture.

## TradeRefer Translation

For TradeRefer, the closest equivalent to Core 30 is not creating 30 pages for every business by default. It is making each profile and local page prove:

- which trade or service the business actually performs,
- where the business genuinely serves,
- what evidence supports the match,
- why a user should contact or request a referral from that business,
- what related service/location pages are canonical and useful.

Profile pages need stronger above-the-fold proof. Local pages need actual businesses, visible service coverage, and trust data, otherwise they should not be indexed.

## Actions For TradeRefer

- Rewrite profile titles/metas so they expose business name, primary trade, suburb/city, and credibility.
- Move review/referral/contact proof higher on profiles where that data exists.
- Add schema validation to ensure LocalBusiness/ProfilePage data only claims what is visible.
- Replace generic local page intros with evidence-led blocks: listed businesses, suburbs served, service match, profile count, review/referral signal, and nearby canonical links.
- Treat broad blog-like content as secondary. The priority is better profile, trade, and local landing architecture.
